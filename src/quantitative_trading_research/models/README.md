# Models Subsystem

## Canonical Responsibility

Own future PPO, Random Forest, XGBoost, training, inference, and model-family implementation responsibilities under the accepted phase sequence.

## Permitted Future Contents

- Deterministic model configuration, training, and inference components after the responsible phase is authorized.
- Immutable model, preprocessing, feature, dataset, environment, and run identity bindings.
- Explicit rejected, inconclusive, and no-candidate outcomes.

## Prohibited Coupling

Do not acquire data, define provider behavior, access the shared final holdout early, promote candidates from migration evidence, connect to brokers, or submit orders.

## Dependency Direction

Models may consume validated configuration, governed data and feature contracts, and artifact identities. Models must not depend on execution.

## Responsible Future Phase

C4 may migrate selected neutral utilities. C8 through C11 govern implementation readiness and candidate-family qualification.

## Required Future Verification

- Verify deterministic configuration and reproducibility.
- Verify chronological validation and leakage-safe inputs.
- Verify model artifacts are immutable and cannot imply qualification without the responsible phase decision.
