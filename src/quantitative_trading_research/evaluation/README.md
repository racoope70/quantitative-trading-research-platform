# Evaluation Subsystem

## Canonical Responsibility

Own chronological evaluation, diagnostics, comparison evidence, candidate qualification support, and shared-final-holdout reporting boundaries.

## Permitted Future Contents

- Offline deterministic diagnostics and parity checks after later authorization.
- Versioned evaluation manifests and explicit terminal outcomes.
- Candidate comparison only under the governing qualification and holdout sequence.

## Prohibited Coupling

Do not mutate training inputs, fit preprocessing on evaluation observations, access the final holdout before C12, treat operational success as economic qualification, or submit orders.

## Dependency Direction

Evaluation may consume immutable data, feature, model, configuration, and artifact identities. Execution must not be required for research evaluation.

## Responsible Future Phase

C4 may prepare selected offline diagnostics. C9 through C12 govern qualification, freezing, comparison, and the shared final holdout.

## Required Future Verification

- Verify chronological boundaries and embargo.
- Verify exact immutable inputs and terminal outcomes.
- Verify final-holdout isolation and one-time governed access.
